This site does not set its own cookies and does not load analytics or marketing services. A consent banner for non-essential cookies is therefore not used. Technically necessary operations by the host (for example server logs processed by GitHub) are separate from that. If this changes, this statement will be updated. The site is not described as “cookie-free” or “GDPR compliant” as a blanket claim.
