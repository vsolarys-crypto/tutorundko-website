## Experience and studies

I have more than ten years of private tutoring experience, mainly from 2013 to 2023. My main subjects are mathematics, chemistry, physics, biology and economics.

From August 2022 to November 2023 I worked as a Science Tutor at cleverly.de. From September 2022 to April 2023 I worked as a teaching and educational assistant at LTPS in Luxembourg.

I studied at the University of Aberdeen from 2016 to 2020 and hold a BSc (Hons) in Neuroscience with Psychology. That is my academic background. It is not a teaching qualification, and it is not a clinical qualification.

## How I teach

I first want to understand how you think. Then we look for an explanation that you can test and use yourself. If a picture helps, we reach for the crayons, at least in our heads. If we need more precision, we go back to the definition.

Questions, mistakes and a return to the basics are welcome. We stay concrete: your syllabus, your exercises and your next steps. My role is not to take every answer off your hands, but to support you in thinking for yourself.

## Languages, place and hours

I teach mainly online, in five languages of instruction: German, English, French, Luxembourgish and Russian, always with an eye on the subject vocabulary you need. German, English, French and Luxembourgish are native languages for me; Russian is available at a business-fluent level, provided the course terminology fits. I do not offer language-learning lessons.

Occasional in-person lessons in Luxembourg or the Trier–Eifel area may be possible by arrangement. Availability and any travel costs are agreed beforehand.

We arrange appointments in the evening window from 18:00 to 21:00 (Europe/Berlin). An enquiry is not a promise that a place is free.

The aim remains understanding, reliable methods and increasingly independent work — not a grade guarantee.
