## How it works

1. **Review your materials.** You send exercises, a syllabus or a module outline, and the language you would like to work in. I check whether I can offer suitable support. Where the learner is under 18, a parent or guardian should be included in the enquiry.
2. **Set a goal.** We agree what we will work on and how you will notice that it is becoming clearer. The goal might be a type of exercise, an exam topic or an uncertain foundation.
3. **Explain and practise.** After a shared explanation, you try the next step yourself. Questions, small changes to the problem and a return to the basics are part of the work.
4. **Look back.** At the end we review what is in place, what is still uncertain and what you can practise before the next lesson.

Lessons are mainly online. Occasional in-person sessions in Luxembourg or the Trier–Eifel area may be possible by arrangement; availability and any travel costs are agreed beforehand.

We arrange appointments between **18:00 and 21:00 (Europe/Berlin)**. An enquiry is not a promise that a place is free.

## Fees

Fees, lesson length and other arrangements are agreed individually on request.

## What I do not promise

The aim is understanding, reliable methods and more independent work. I do not guarantee particular grades or exam results, and I do not claim official accreditation as a teacher.
