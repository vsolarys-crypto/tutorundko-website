I support learners from primary-school foundations through secondary-school graduation, including preparation that follows your Abitur, Bac, A-level or Luxembourg syllabus. For first-year university topics, please send your materials first so we can check that the content is a good fit. I do not claim coverage of a whole degree, and I do not claim exam-board accreditation.

## Mathematics {#mathe}

I offer maths support from school foundations through graduation, with selected first-year university topics after I have reviewed the syllabus. Depending on your needs, we can work on calculation, algebra, functions, geometry or statistics. We revisit the ideas, clarify methods and apply them to your exercises. For Abitur, Bac or A-level preparation we use your materials.

## Chemistry {#chemie}

In chemistry we connect terminology, particle models, equations and calculations. We work from your course materials: school foundations through graduation, and selected first-year topics after a syllabus check. We discuss coefficients, quantities and reaction equations so that you can justify the next step yourself. I do not recommend hazardous home experiments.

## Physics {#physik}

A physics problem often starts with a sketch and a reasoned expectation, not immediately with a formula. Depending on your course, we can practise mechanics, electricity, energy or other school topics using your exercises. We pay attention to quantities, units and the limits of a model. Selected first-year university topics are possible after I have reviewed your materials.

## Biology {#biologie}

I help learners understand biology rather than memorise disconnected lists. Depending on your course, we can explore cells, genetics, physiology or relationships between organisms and their environment. My BSc (Hons) in Neuroscience with Psychology from the University of Aberdeen (2016–2020) complements this science tutoring. We use diagrams, questions and practice tasks to build accurate explanations. Please send first-year materials in advance so we can check the fit.

## Economics {#wirtschaft}

I offer economics support through secondary-school graduation, and selected introductory university topics after checking your syllabus. My International Baccalaureate studies at Athénée de Luxembourg included Economics at Higher Level. That is school-level subject knowledge, not a qualification as an economist. Depending on your course, we can work on supply and demand, market mechanisms, economic indicators or interpreting diagrams. We clarify definitions, connect models to examples and practise building a reasoned answer.

## Neuroscience and psychology {#neuro}

Alongside the main subjects I offer introductory academic support in neuroscience and psychology. This draws on my BSc (Hons) in Neuroscience with Psychology from the University of Aberdeen (2016–2020). Depending on your syllabus, we can revisit nervous-system function, cognitive psychology, research methods or statistics. Please share your materials beforehand so we can confirm that the topics are a suitable fit.

This is educational tutoring, not psychological counselling, diagnosis or treatment.
